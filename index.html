Do I have to switch my software?

No. The first workflow is built around the phone, CRM and calendar you already use.

What does the first project look like?

One workflow aimed at your biggest leak, such as missed-call text-back or estimate follow-up. It goes live, gets measured, and only then do we talk about a second one.

Who runs it once it's live?

MindVault does. You don't need someone on staff to manage it, and you get a weekly brief on what it handled.

You choose where you want to approve messages and where it can reply on its own. Anything unusual gets handed to a person.
