Feedback and Testimonials

Our testimonial form collects your name, email address, optional business name, feedback, and your website-publication permission choice. We record the wording and version of the permission request and the submission time. Your submission is stored privately in our U.S.-hosted Supabase database for review. Submitting feedback does not subscribe you to marketing.

Permission to publish is optional and unchecked by default. Without it, your feedback stays private. If you grant permission, we may manually feature your testimonial on trustmvs.com with the name and business you provided; your email is never published. Nothing publishes automatically. Permission does not cover your photo or company logo.

To request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of testimonial permission, email jb@trustmvs.com. Include your submission receipt if available. We retain unpublished feedback only while needed for review and follow-up, and approved testimonials while the permission and business purpose remain valid.
